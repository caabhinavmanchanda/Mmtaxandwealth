About Manisha

Manisha is a Chartered Accountant and senior corporate indirect tax leader with over a decade of specialised experience across GST compliance, strategic advisory and corporate litigation. She has managed high-stakes financial portfolios and optimised indirect tax frameworks for major global and domestic enterprises spanning retail, e-commerce, manufacturing and real estate.

She has spearheaded critical tax operations at Walmart, driven digital travel tax compliance at Yatra Online Limited, managed complex corporate tax structures at DCM Shriram Ltd, where she was also selected for executive leadership mentoring, and led the indirect tax vertical at Brookfield Properties.

She now brings that corporate-side rigour directly to clients as founder of M&M Tax and Wealth Advisors. Her expertise spans cross-functional tax planning, drafting definitive legal positions, and leading multi-state GST audits through India's post-GST transition — for founders and finance leaders who want one advisor across every filing, not a different specialist for each.

What we handle

Thirteen practice areas, run by one team. Open a category to see exactly what's included — from a monthly GSTR-3B to a cross-border transfer pricing filing.

GST compliance & advisory
  • End-to-end compliance management. Monthly, quarterly and annual GST returns — GSTR-1, 3B, 4 — filed with zero errors.
  • Annual GST audits. Compiling and filing annual returns and reconciliation statements under GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C.
  • Departmental notice solutions. Drafting watertight replies to GST notices, managing scrutiny and leading assessments.
  • Refunds and registrations. Swift GST refund filing, export claims and seamless multi-state new registrations.
Income tax & tax planning
  • Strategic salary tax planning. Optimising personal salary structures to legal limits to minimise income tax liability.
  • Capital gains optimisation. Advisory on capital gains from property, shares and mutual funds.
  • Business ITR & compliance. Preparation, computation and filing of complex business income tax returns.
  • Trust & society taxation. Exemption registration and compliance tracking for NGOs and trusts.
Wealth management & advisory
  • Custom financial planning. Personalised, goal-based blueprints for sustainable wealth accumulation.
  • Investment portfolio advisory. Independent analysis and performance tracking of mutual fund investments.
  • Retirement & estate management. Long-term retirement timelines built toward financial independence.
  • Risk & insurance planning. Assessing risk profiles to recommend life and health cover.
Corporate & business services
  • New company incorporation. Private Limited, LLP and OPC registrations with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
  • Annual ROC compliance. Board resolutions, annual forms and mandatory ROC filings.
  • Startup India recognition. Securing DPIIT certificates to unlock tax holidays and government benefits.
  • MSME & government registration. Fast-tracked MSME/Udyam registration for credit access and subsidies.
Auditing & assurance
  • Statutory audits. Independent examination of corporate financial records for regulatory compliance.
  • Internal risk audits. Reviewing internal controls to detect leakages and improve efficiency.
  • Income tax audits. Mandated tax audits under Section 44AB ahead of filing deadlines.
Loan & corporate financing
  • Project finance & term loans. Detailed project reports and funding for capital-intensive expansion.
  • Working capital & CC limits. Cash credit limits, bank guarantees and overdraft facilities.
  • MSME & business loans. Collateral-free loans and subsidised government funding for small enterprises.
  • Home & property loans. Documentation for loans against property and residential home loans.
Trademark & MSME services
  • Trademark registration. Brand availability searches and filing official trademark applications.
  • Objection & opposition handling. Drafting responses and representing clients in show-cause hearings.
  • MSME / Udyam registration. Certificates enabling priority lending and lower interest rates.
  • Government subsidy advisory. Identifying eligible subsidies, tender preferences and payment protections.
NRI taxation & remittances
  • NRI tax return filing. Indian income tax returns for non-resident Indians with local income or assets.
  • Form 15CA & 15CB certification. CA certificates for outward foreign fund remittances.
  • DTAA benefit advisory. Structuring cross-border earnings to claim treaty tax credits.
  • FEMA compliance & setup. Guidance on inward investment, NRE/NRO accounts and exchange law.
Virtual CFO & growth advisory
  • Outsourced CFO services. Strategic financial leadership, cash flow and profitability advisory.
  • Advanced MIS reporting. Monthly performance dashboards for financial clarity.
  • Budgeting & variance analysis. Operational budgets tracked monthly against actuals.
  • Cost optimisation checks. Controls that plug leakages and improve working capital cycles.
International tax & transfer pricing
  • Transfer pricing audits. Form 3CEB filings and local documentation to comply with regulations.
  • Cross-border transaction taxing. Advisory on withholding tax, royalties and expatriate tax planning.
  • BEPS & POEM advisory. Navigating base erosion and place-of-effective-management rules.
Payroll & labour compliance
  • End-to-end payroll processing. Salary computation, payslips and tax-efficient CTC models.
  • PF & ESI management. Monthly deposits, employee registration and timely return filing.
  • Labour law filings. Computing liabilities for gratuity, bonuses and statutory compliance.
Startup valuation & fundraising
  • Business valuation certificates. Reports using discounted cash flow and net asset methods.
  • Financial projection modelling. Five-year models built for investor scrutiny.
  • Investor due diligence readiness. Structuring records for a seamless due diligence process.
Digital assets & crypto taxation
  • Crypto & VDA tax filing. Computing and filing tax on virtual digital assets, NFTs and staking income.
  • TDS on crypto transactions. Guidance on the 1% TDS rule under Section 194S.
  • Portfolio advisory. Tax implications of high-volume digital asset holdings for HNIs.
